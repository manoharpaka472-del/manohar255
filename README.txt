REAL PHOTOS FOR YOUR PRODUCTS
=============================
Drop your own photos in this folder using EXACTLY these names
(.jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp):

  cloth_dryer.jpg            Wall & Ceiling Mounted Cloth Dryer
  cooling_enclosure.jpg      Passive Cooling Enclosure
  street_light.jpg           Automatic Street Light
  soil_moisture_alarm.jpg    Soil Moisture Alarm
  energy_meter.jpg           IoT-Based Energy Meter
  solar_tracker.jpg          Solar Tracker System
  studentapppro.jpg          StudentAppPro (a screenshot works well)

Each product card then shows your photo first, with a "View 3D" button
to switch to the interactive 3D model.

No photo yet? Instead of a file, you can paste an image link for that
product into image_urls.json. Only use photos you took or that are free to
use, and credit the photographer if the licence asks for it.
Cards with neither a file nor a link simply show the 3D model.
